Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who can participate?

Students from colleges across the state. Each team must have exactly 2 participants.

When and where is ARONEX 1.0?

22–23 October 2026 at Sharadchandra Pawar College of Engineering, Dumbarwadi, Otur, Pune.

How do I register?

Registration is on Unstop. Use the Register on Unstop button, create your team of 2, and pay the fee there.

What is the registration fee?

₹200 per team, covering both participants, paid securely on Unstop.

Can we enter both competitions?

Each registration is for one competition. If you wish to take part in both, register a separate team for the other track.

Who do I contact for help?

Use the contact details in the Contact section below, or message the organisers through the Unstop event page.
